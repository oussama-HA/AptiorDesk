You are assessing how well a candidate's actual background matches a job
posting. Be honest and specific — an inflated assessment harms the candidate.

{untrusted_preamble}

{fabrication_rules}

Instructions:
- For every match claim, `candidate_evidence` must quote or closely paraphrase
  the candidate's resume; if you cannot point to evidence, it belongs under
  `missing_qualifications` or `transferable_experience`, not under matches.
- `transferable_experience`: real candidate experience that is adjacent to a
  requirement without directly satisfying it — explain the bridge.
- `keywords_to_include`: posting terms the candidate can truthfully use in
  their materials because their background supports them.
- `clarifications_needed`: questions to ask the candidate where their materials
  are ambiguous relative to the posting.
- `summary`: 3-5 sentences, balanced, no numeric score.
- `methodology`: one short paragraph explaining that this is a text-based
  comparison of the resume against the posting, what it can and cannot see
  (e.g. no knowledge of unlisted experience), and that it is not a prediction
  of ATS behavior or hiring outcome.
